# Welcome to PainPilot

PainPilot turns internet complaints into shipped, paying AI micro-SaaS for solo founders.

The loop in one paragraph: crawlers from Reddit / HN / Product Hunt / App Store / Play Store / Trustpilot / G2 / Indie Hackers stream public complaints in. Voyage embeddings + cosine-threshold pgvector clustering group them into demand clusters. Once you save a cluster you care about, an LLM pipeline streams a full product spec — audience, competitors, willingness-to-pay, pricing, MVP feature list, GTM hypothesis. Lock the spec; PainPilot generates the MVP into a fresh GitHub repo via its App, optionally deploys to Vercel, and wires Polar marketplace billing with a 10% revenue share. We never auto-post to social networks — every launch asset (PH, X, cold email, Reddit replies) is produced as a DRAFT for you to send yourself.
